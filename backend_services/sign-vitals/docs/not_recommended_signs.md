# Dictionary-only signs (not recommended)

Five signs (me, good, stand, welcome and arm) are never chosen automatically by GLOSS's lesson recommendations, because in their reference videos the signer's hands are barely or only briefly visible to MediaPipe, so their animated demonstrations are unreliable. They remain in the sign dictionary with a note to compare carefully with the 2D guide, can be practised and graded exactly like any other sign, and keep their existing grading, mastery and history.

Configuration: `sign_config.py` (`NOT_RECOMMENDED`, with a reason per sign). This list is separate from the model-unreliable list (hurt, pain, me, sleep), which concerns recognition accuracy rather than demonstration quality.
